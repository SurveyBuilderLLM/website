A few practical questions.

Is Aayvu available now?+

We're in early access, onboarding research teams in waves. Request access above; it usually takes just a few days.

What is an AI survey builder?+

An AI survey builder turns a questionnaire or research brief into a working survey. Aayvu programs the question types, answer options, branching logic, piping, validation, translations, and quotas, then keeps the full build editable for human review.

Can I review changes before publishing?+

Yes. Every question, logic rule, validation, and agent-made change remains visible and editable before launch.
