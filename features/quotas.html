01 Go deeper

The right respondents, in the right numbers.

Cap completes by any segment — age, region, behavior, anything you ask. When a quota fills, new respondents are screened gracefully instead of hitting a dead end.

Watch quotas fill live while fieldwork runs, and adjust on the fly.

Two modesHard quotas stop at the target. Soft quotas keep counting.

02 Why it matters

Control composition while fieldwork moves.

01

Define the audience precisely

Map a quota to a specific question and answer rather than maintaining a separate sample sheet.

02

Choose what happens at the limit

Stop respondents with a hard quota or continue collecting with a soft one.

03

Keep the exit understandable

Use a dedicated quota-full message instead of leaving respondents at a dead end.

03 In practice

Map the answer to the target.

Quota setup stays close to the survey logic that decides who counts.

  1. 01

    Define

    Name the segment, set the completion limit, and choose hard or soft behavior.

  2. 02

    Map

    Connect the quota to the qualifying answer or apply it through an answer action.

  3. 03

    Monitor

    Watch completes against the target and adjust the setup as fieldwork changes.
