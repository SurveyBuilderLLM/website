03 In practice

Walk every important path.

A good QA pass combines automatic checks with a human run-through of the respondent experience.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Analyze the questionnaire for broken references, conflicting actions, and invalid configuration.

  2. 02

    Run

    Answer as a respondent and deliberately trigger screeners, branches, quotas, and validations.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Return to the flagged rule, make the change, and repeat the path before publishing.
