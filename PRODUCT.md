# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Repository-inferred: market-research teams, including researchers and survey programmers, who need to turn research briefs or questionnaires into field-ready surveys and then review, test, translate, launch, and manage them.

## Product Purpose

Aayvu is an AI-powered research suite intended to reduce manual survey programming work. Success means a team can move from a source document or brief to a reviewable, launch-ready study while retaining control over questions, logic, validation, and respondent paths.

## Positioning

Repository-inferred: Aayvu combines document ingestion, AI-assisted survey programming, a visual/editorial review workflow, respondent-path testing, and downstream study operations in one research-specific product.

## Operating Context

Teams work from Word documents, PDFs, research briefs, questionnaires, recurring trackers, and multi-market study specifications. The marketing site demonstrates programming, branching logic, piping, validation, respondent preview, translation, quota management, theming, QA, and administration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript marketing site hosted on Cloudflare Pages.
- Homepage and 15 feature/use-case pages share navigation, theme behavior, and generated subpage templates.
- Light and dark themes must remain supported.
- Existing interactions, keyboard behavior, reduced-motion handling, and responsive layouts must remain functional.
- Product claims marked as placeholders or needing verification in `README.md` must not be strengthened or presented as established customer proof.

- Existing amber/orange brand family and current Aayvu logo assets are established repository commitments.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product and deployment notes in `README.md`.

## Product Principles

- Make complex research operations feel legible and controllable.
- Demonstrate the workflow instead of relying on unsupported claims.
- Preserve researcher judgment while automating repetitive programming work.

- Communicate with precise, research-literate language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Repository-inferred: preserve semantic HTML, visible focus, keyboard-accessible controls, responsive behavior, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and usable touch targets.
