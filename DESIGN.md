# Aayvu Design System

## Direction

**Research instrument manual.** Aayvu should feel like the clearest, most modern fieldwork manual a research team has ever used: systematic enough to trust, calm enough to invite exploration, and visibly built around real survey artifacts rather than generic AI spectacle.

The visual world combines the disciplined component language of the referenced Simple Design System with Aayvu's existing editorial workbench. Cool mineral surfaces, precise rules, amber signals, compact metadata, and purpose-built product demonstrations form one system across the homepage, detail pages, contact flow, navigation, and footer.

## Foundations

- **Canvas:** cool mineral gray-green, derived from research-lab equipment and document scanners rather than generic cream paper.
- **Ink:** near-black brown for text; high contrast without a cold software feel.
- **Signal:** Aayvu amber is reserved for active states, important metadata, and conversion actions.
- **Supporting hue:** muted moss for verified/healthy states; no rainbow UI.
- **Type:** Geologica for interface and editorial display, using its technical variable axes for personality; IBM Plex Mono for labels, counters, file names, and research metadata.
- **Geometry:** 10–18px radii for interactive surfaces; hairline borders; shadows are soft and low, never floating glass.
- **Icons:** consistent 1.75px line icons from Iconify-compatible Lucide/Tabler families. Icons clarify actions or categories; they are not decorative confetti.

## Composition

- Use a 12-column editorial grid and strong alignment between prose and product demonstrations.
- Alternate quiet paper sections with contained instrument panels instead of undifferentiated full-width bands.
- Keep headings compact, balanced, and readable; body copy stays under 68 characters per line where practical.
- Product proof is interactive and specific. Unsupported customer logos, metrics, and testimonials are forbidden.

## Components

- **Navigation:** compact floating rail on wide screens, full-width anchored bar on small screens.
- **Buttons:** substantial but not oversized; 10px corners; clear primary/secondary hierarchy; icon motion no more than 2px.
- **Panels:** warm white surface, visible border, inset highlight, subtle grounded shadow.
- **Metadata:** mono uppercase, 11–12px, increased tracking, amber or muted ink.
- **Forms:** 48px minimum control height, clear labels, visible focus, helpful copy adjacent to the decision.
- **Tabs:** segmented instrument controls with unmistakable selected state and keyboard focus.

## Motion

Motion explains state changes: panels reveal, progress advances, and arrows acknowledge hover. Avoid ambient motion that competes with reading. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and preserve static meaning.

## Responsive Behavior

Desktop composition may be asymmetric; mobile becomes a clean single-column document. Horizontal tools may scroll only when their semantics remain obvious. Touch targets are at least 44px. Product demos simplify before text becomes illegible.

## Accessibility

Maintain semantic landmarks and heading order, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast in both themes, descriptive labels, reduced-motion behavior, and non-color state cues.
